# Code of conduct

turri follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, in its issues, pull requests and discussions, on its Discord, and wherever someone speaks for the project.

In short: be kind and patient, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas rather than people, and accept that the maintainer may decline a change. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

If someone's behaviour concerns you, write to the maintainer at [zan@codeplain.ai](mailto:zan@codeplain.ai). Reports are read by no one else and kept confidential; the covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) say what may follow.
